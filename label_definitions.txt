analysis: makes a point and backs it with at least one specific, checkable fact offered as support: a number, a date, a named person or title, or a quoted line
hot_take: states a judgment or claim someone could disagree with, about a film, a person, or the industry, without offering a specific, checkable fact to support it
reaction: expresses a feeling, or just names a title or line, without making a claim anyone could argue with
